Doodle Studio

Privacy policy

Last updated September 27, 2026. This covers the Doodle Studio app and its Doodle Cloud AI director. The Chrome extension for teachers has its own policy.

Doodle Studio turns a script you write into a hand-drawn video on your own computer. The app, the site and Doodle Cloud have no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing about you is ever sold.

The offline director: nothing leaves your computer

With the offline director you need no account. Your scripts, projects, narration and videos stay on your computer. The app only downloads its voice model (from GitHub) and doodle-search model (from Hugging Face) the first time they are needed; those downloads carry nothing about you.

Doodle Cloud, the AI director (optional)

If you sign in with your email and choose "Doodle Cloud AI", this is what is sent, and to whom:

WhereWhatWhy
Doodle Cloud (api.doodlecloud.org, run by Doodle Studio)your email, a random ID for this installation of the app, then each section's sentences, its title and the names of candidate doodlesto sign you in, count your free videos, and ask GPT-6 Luna for picture suggestions
Resend (email delivery)your email address and a 6-digit sign-in codeto email you the code
OpenAI (GPT-6 Luna), through Doodle Cloudthe same sentences, title and doodle namesto suggest pictures; OpenAI's API terms apply
Cloudflare (hosting)ordinary request data, such as your IP addressto run the service and limit abuse

Doodle Cloud stores your email, your plan, the install ID, counts of your videos and their AI cost, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your sign-in token, never the token itself. It never stores your script, the suggestions, your narration or your video. A Gmail address counts as one account however it is written (with dots or a "+tag").

Sign-in codes are deleted when used, or within about a day after they expire. Rate-limit records, which contain an IP address or email, are deleted within about two days. On your computer, the sign-in token is kept in your system's keychain.

Deleting your account

To delete your Doodle Cloud account and its usage counts, email privacy@doodlecloud.org from the address you signed in with.

Children

Doodle Cloud accounts are not meant for children under 13. Anyone can use the offline director, which needs no account and sends nothing.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its date change with it. Questions: the project's GitHub page or privacy@doodlecloud.org.
